Jobhunt Studio

Paste a job ad. It checks every requirement against your CV, scores the fit out of 10, then tailors an honest CV and cover letter.

  • It refuses to invent. A "facts and limits" list overrides the CV at every step, so the model cannot claim a skill you don't have. Gaps are shown.
  • No server, no account. A static page with a strict content security policy. Your CV and API key never leave your browser except to the model you chose.
  • Any model. Claude, OpenAI, OpenRouter, a local model through Ollama, or any compatible API.
  • Small and tested. About 2,800 lines of plain JavaScript and CSS, no framework, no build step, 33 unit tests, and a privacy check that runs in CI.

A startup intelligence platform. It gathers signals from across the web, runs AI agents to find and score young companies, and shows the result in one dashboard.
